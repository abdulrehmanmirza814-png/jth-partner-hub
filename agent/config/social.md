# Social media voice and content ideas
(The assistant reads this before drafting Facebook/Instagram posts. Edit freely.)

## Voice
Warm, helpful, local expert. Short sentences. Friendly but professional. Never pushy.

## Content pillars (rotate)
1. Experience spotlight: one desert safari / dhow cruise / city tour moment and what guests enjoy about it.
2. Tips for visitors: best time to visit, what to wear, what to bring, how pickups work.
3. Behind the scenes: our vehicles, our drivers, how Abdul looks after each guest personally.
4. Guest moments (only with permission, no names/faces without consent).
5. Seasonal: weather, events, public holidays in the UAE.

## Always end with one clear action
"Message us on WhatsApp for a quick quote" or "See our tours: <your website>"
