Find Facebook pages and public groups about UAE / Dubai tourism and travel, and leave genuinely relevant comments on their recent posts that mention JTH Tourism and our website.

How to do it:
1. Use the Facebook search box to search for: "Dubai tourism", "UAE travel", "Dubai tours", "desert safari Dubai". Open the Pages results (not people).
2. Open a page, look at its latest posts (scroll a little).
3. Only comment on a post if it is clearly about tourism, travel or things to do in the UAE, and is positive or neutral. Skip anything controversial, political, a complaint, a tragedy, an advert for a competitor, or in a group whose rules forbid promotion.
4. Write a short, friendly, specific comment (1-2 sentences) that reacts to what that post actually shows, and mentions JTH Tourism with our website https://YOUR_WEBSITE_URL once, for example offering help with desert safaris, city tours or transport. Every comment must be different. Never copy a previous comment.
5. Use the post's URL as the dedupe_key so we never comment on the same post twice.
6. If you hit a login wall, a warning, a captcha, or "try again later", stop and ask the owner.

Stop when the publish limits are reached.
